# Group play: Versus with nearby consoles

Play Versus with up to **four New 3DS consoles in the same room**, each player on their own console. Group play uses
the 3DS's local wireless, like Download Play or local multiplayer in retail games: **no internet, no account and no
router.**

Group play is part of both regular builds (everything unlocked and fresh save), and the two can play together.

## What you need

- Two to four **New 3DS** consoles, each with Melee for New 3DS installed (either build).
- **Wireless communication turned on** on every console (the wireless switch on older models, or the HOME Menu
  settings).
- Every console on the **same version** of the game. Build them from the same release.

## Host a game

1. Choose **VS Mode → Melee**.
2. On the bottom screen, tap **Group**. The console starts looking for games nearby.
3. Tap **HOST GAME**.

Your console opens the lobby (the character select screen) and other consoles can now find your game.

## Join a game

1. Choose **VS Mode → Melee**, then tap **Group** on the bottom screen.
2. The **Available Games** list shows the games nearby: the host's name and Mii, and a stock icon for each fighter
   already in the lobby. When more games are found than fit, use the arrows beside the page number.
3. Tap a game to join it.

You land in the host's lobby. To play on your own instead, tap **Solo** on the first page, or press **B** to go back.

## The lobby

The lobby is Melee's character select screen, shared by every console. The four doors are the four ports: **port 1 is
the host**, and each console that joins takes the next free port.

- **Pick your fighter, costume and name tag** as usual.
- **Anyone can add a CPU** or close a door, including doors no console sits in. A door that someone else is using (a
  slider, the name tag list, name entry) can't be changed until they finish.
- **Only the host sets the rules** (stocks, time, items, Teams, handicap). The other consoles show the host's rules on
  their bottom screen.
- The bottom screen shows who is connected, each console's **signal strength**, and what each player is doing.
  A short message appears when someone joins or leaves.
- Each player's name (their Mii name, or the console's name) is shown under their door and above their damage in the
  match.
- Moving the **C-stick** turns the character select camera on every console, just for fun.
- **Start** works on any console once every door in use has a fighter. It is disabled while the host is in the rules
  menu.

### The stage select

Any player can pick the stage, and the first pick counts for everyone. If the host picks a stage your save hasn't
unlocked yet, your console shows "Now loading" without revealing the stage, and you play on it anyway. Fighters you
haven't unlocked work the same way.

## Matches

Every console runs the same match in step. The game waits for the slowest console, so if one console slows down
(for example four fighters on a large stage), everyone slows down with it.

- **Sudden death** and **Teams** work as usual.
- If a player's console disconnects or leaves during a match, **their fighter becomes a CPU** and the match goes on.
- After the match, every console shows **"The winner is..."** and the results together, with each player's name under
  their window. The results move on when every player has pressed through them. Then everyone returns to the lobby
  for the next match.
- Stars and auto handicap work as in normal Versus.

## Joining during a match

You can join a game whose match has already started. Your bottom screen then shows the **match in progress**: the
timer, and each player's stocks and damage. You join the lobby when the match ends.

## Leaving

- **Press B on the character select screen** to leave the lobby. If you are the host and other players are there, you
  are asked to confirm first.
- **When the host leaves, the session ends for everyone.** The other consoles go back to the Group page, which says why
  the session ended (the host left, stopped responding, and so on).
- If a console loses the connection, it goes back to the Group page with the reason.

## Your save

Each console uses its own save. Nobody else's progress is copied to yours, and your unlocks don't change.

Group matches count toward your **total VS matches and play time**. They don't write **VS Records**, and they don't
give trophies or trigger a "new challenger" fight.

## Good to know

- **The HOME button is off** while you are in a group (browsing, the lobby, a match or its results). Pressing it shows
  "HOME IS UNAVAILABLE DURING WIRELESS PLAY". Leave the group to use HOME.
- **Closing the lid doesn't put the console to sleep** while you are in a group, so nobody gets disconnected. It sleeps
  normally again once you leave.
- Keep the consoles close together. Walls and distance weaken the signal; the bars on the bottom screen show how good
  each connection is.
- **The music may pause briefly on the Available Games list** while the console looks for games and draws the hosts'
  Miis. This is a known issue.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please enable wireless and try again" | Turn on wireless communication (the switch, or the HOME Menu settings), then tap Group again |
| The host's game doesn't appear | Make sure the host tapped HOST GAME and is on the character select screen; move closer; check both consoles run the same version |
| A game says FULL | Four consoles are already in it. Wait for someone to leave, or host your own game |
| The session ends during play | The host left or lost the connection. Move closer and host again |
| No Mii next to a name | The console has no Mii set up (or it couldn't be read). The game shows the Smash emblem instead |

To report a problem, include `SD:/3ds/melee/game.log` from **every** console in the session, copied right after it
happened, and the steps that led to it. See [Reporting problems](../../README.md#reporting-problems).
