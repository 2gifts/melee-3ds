Melee for New 3DS - Slippi Direct BETA builder
==============================================

This makes the Slippi Direct beta of Melee for New 3DS from YOUR OWN copy
of Super Smash Bros. Melee. With it, your New 3DS can play 1v1 online
against a friend on Slippi Dolphin (PC), using connect codes (Direct).
Nothing from the game is included in this download.

THIS IS A BETA. It installs NEXT TO the regular Melee for New 3DS (its own
HOME Menu app with a green icon) and never replaces it. Please read
"Slippi beta guide.txt" (in this folder): it explains how to play and how
the experience differs from Slippi on a PC. In short, the 3DS has no
rollback: it briefly waits when your friend's inputs arrive late.

This is an unofficial fan project, not made or supported by Project Slippi.
Please do not ask the Slippi team for help with it.

What you need
-------------
- A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit) with an internet connection.
  (Not Mac, Linux, Steam Deck, Wine or a phone.)
- About 8 GB of free disk space.
- Your Melee disc image: the US version 1.02, as a plain .iso file (1.4 GB).
  A .rvz or .gcz file can be turned into an .iso with Dolphin: right-click
  the game > Convert File... > Uncompressed Disc (ISO).
- A New 3DS, New 3DS XL or New 2DS XL with custom firmware and FBI.
- A Slippi account (log in to Slippi Launcher on a PC), and a friend with
  their OWN Slippi account on Slippi Launcher.

How to build
------------
1. Extract this whole zip first: right-click it > Extract All... > Extract.
   (Do not run the builder from inside the zip.)
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Build Melee Slippi Beta.bat"
   (or drag your .iso onto it).
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".
3. Choose your Melee .iso when asked, then wait. The first build takes
   10 to 30 minutes. Leave the window open. You can use your PC meanwhile.
4. If you are logged in to Slippi Launcher on this PC, the builder offers
   to put that account on the SD card. Type Y to accept. The account file
   (user.json) is your Slippi login: never share it or post it.
5. When it finishes, a folder opens with your files. Follow
   "What to do next.txt" in that folder.

If the build stops
------------------
Read the yellow message: it says what to do. Then run the builder again;
it continues where it stopped. Common fixes:
- "internet" or "download": check your connection and try again.
- "certificate" or "date": set your PC's date and time correctly
  (Settings > Time & language > Date & time > Set time automatically).
- "space": free up disk space (empty the Recycle Bin).
- "blocked" or "antivirus": allow the MeleeSlippiBuild folder in your
  antivirus.

The builder works in C:\MeleeSlippiBuild (or another folder with enough
space; the window shows where), separate from the regular builder's
C:\MeleeBuild. You can delete it once your files are on the SD card, or
keep it to make future updates faster. If you let the builder add your
Slippi account, that folder contains your login: delete it if others use
this PC.
